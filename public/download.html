XXYRO BROWSE

A quieter place to browse.

Choose the version for your device. Desktop downloads run XyroBrowse with its bundled Chromium engine, so ordinary websites do not depend on the hosted Lite Mode proxy.

Desktop is the full browser. Android installation currently uses the web app (Lite Mode); it is not yet a native Chromium-based Android build.
